## PHI-048 — Season commitments: evidence at incident time, principles after recurrence

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Domain:** process
**Source Project:** Oracle
**Source:** Full-corpus review of the Decision Oracle found the recent capture batch minting philosophies at incident+0 with 1:1 OBS mirror pairs, routing around the graduation lifecycle.

### Philosophy
Capture evidence the moment an incident happens, but mint commitments — PHIs, principles, policies — only after recurrence: a second incident, a second project, or a retrieval hit proving demand. A principle minted at incident+0 is a hypothesis wearing a conclusion's clothes.

### Why I Hold This
Incident-fresh principles inherit one project's framing, pair 1:1 with their single evidence record, and inflate the corpus faster than reflection can use it. The observation→graduation lifecycle exists precisely to season these; minting directly defeats the quality gate. Older PHIs that generalized across recurrences aged better than the July 2026 batch minted same-week from two projects.

### Evidence
- OBS-032 — 2026-07-06 Oracle corpus review: 23 entries in 20 days vs ~9 queries served; ~9 of 23 were 1:1 PHI/OBS mirror pairs of single incidents; graduation ran once ever (supports; retained this session).
- PHI-038 dangling cross-reference (corrected 2026-07-06, PR #27): a PHI minted at incident+0 cited an OBS twin that was never actually retained — the mirror-pair habit produced a phantom reference (supports).

### Where It Applies
Decision oracles and knowledge banks, ADR/postmortem→policy pipelines, style guides, team norm-setting — anywhere incident learnings are promoted into standing rules.

### Known Tensions
Seasoning adds latency: a genuinely structural insight may deserve immediate commitment when waiting means repeating the incident. PHI-003's conscious-capture tradeoff already accepts coverage loss; this adds a second filter on top. Recurrence can be gamed by citing near-duplicate incidents.

### Open to Revision When
Seasoned observations routinely die awaiting graduation while their unminted principles would have prevented real incidents; or retrieval-side filtering becomes reliable enough that corpus inflation stops degrading recall quality.
