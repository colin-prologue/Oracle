## OBS-032 — Oracle corpus review: capture outpaced retrieval
**Relationship:** supports PHI-048

On 2026-07-06, a full-corpus review of the Decision Oracle (Oracle project) quantified a capture/retrieval decoupling: 78 retained entries, of which 45 (58%) had never been retrieved or cited in any logged query; 23 entries were added June 15–July 5 while only ~9 queries were served in the same window; ~60% of all 47 logged queries were about the oracle system itself; ~9 of the 23 recent entries were 1:1 PHI/OBS mirror pairs of single same-week incidents from two projects (Switchboard, rts-proto); the OBS→PHI graduation mechanism had run exactly once (2026-06-12). Retrieval frequency tracked embedding proximity to asked questions, not entry quality — two of the best-written entries (PHI-009, PHI-013) had never surfaced. The review also identified seven merge clusters (~30% of the corpus as facets of one enforcement/verification/durability meta-principle) as the baseline for a future consolidation and retirement pass.
